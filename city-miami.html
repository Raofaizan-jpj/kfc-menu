Ordering KFC in Miami: Bilingual Service, Late Nights & Florida Tax

Miami's multicultural dining scene combines late-night beach cravings with quick family meals. Key consumer notes:

  • Miami-Dade Sales Tax: Orders in Miami are subject to a combined 7.00% sales tax (6.0% Florida state + 1.0% Miami-Dade discretionary surtax).
  • Bilingual Ordering: Digital kiosks, drive-thru attendants, and mobile apps fully support both English and Spanish (Menú y Precios de KFC en Estados Unidos).
  • Late Night Delivery: South Beach and Calle Ocho locations operate drive-thrus and DoorDash / Uber Eats delivery until 2:00 AM.
