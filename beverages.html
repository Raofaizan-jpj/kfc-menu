Exclusive Fountain Drinks, Cold Drink Prices & Sweet Treats

In 2019, PepsiCo and KFC partnered to engineer a signature beverage crafted specifically to complement the savory, spiced profile of Colonel's fried chicken: MTN DEW Sweet Lightning. With its distinct amber-gold color and smooth blend of sweet peach and honey flavors, it remains an exclusive highlight available only at KFC fountain dispensers.
