Combo FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions About KFC Combos & Meals

A standard KFC meal combo includes your main chicken entree (such as a Chicken Sandwich, 2-pc or 3-pc bone-in chicken, or 4-5 hand-breaded tenders), an individual homestyle side (like Secret Recipe Fries or Mashed Potatoes with Gravy), a warm buttermilk biscuit (included with chicken pieces and tenders), and a 20 oz medium fountain beverage.
KFC combo meals in the US generally cost between $8.99 and $11.99 depending on the entree choice and local market pricing. For instance, the Chicken Sandwich Combo is priced at $9.49, the 2-Piece Drum & Thigh Combo is $8.99, the 3-Piece Chicken Combo is $10.99, and the 5-Piece Tenders Combo is $11.49.
Yes. At no additional charge, you can substitute fries for Mashed Potatoes with Gravy, Creamy Coleslaw, Sweet Kernel Corn, or Seasoned Green Beans. Upgrading to Mac & Cheese may involve a slight upcharge of $0.30 to $0.50 depending on the location.
A standard Combo includes 1 main entree, 1 side, 1 biscuit, and 1 drink. A Big Box (or KFC signature box) adds an extra side or side item (like 2 sides + biscuit + drink). For pairs, KFC offers 2-person meal bundles (often referred to as a duo box or 2 Can Dine deal) containing 2 sandwiches or 4 chicken pieces, 2 sides, 2 biscuits, and 2 drinks for around $16.99 to $18.99.
The lowest-priced individual meal options on the national menu are the Taste of KFC $4.99 2-Piece Deal (Drumstick & Thigh + Mashed Potatoes + Biscuit) and the 2-Piece Chicken Littles Combo ($7.49).
The Famous Bowl is sold a la carte for $6.99. You can upgrade to a Famous Bowl Combo (which adds a 20 oz medium drink and a chocolate chip cookie or buttermilk biscuit) for $8.99.
