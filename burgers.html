The Anatomy of the KFC Chicken Sandwich

Introduced nationwide to critical acclaim, the KFC Chicken Sandwich is built around an extra-thick, 100% white-meat chicken breast filet. Every filet is double-hand-breaded in KFC's signature crispy breading to achieve an exceptionally crunchy exterior while locking in natural juiciness.

KFC Warm Tortilla Wraps (2 for $5.00 Deal)

For on-the-go lunches, KFC US locations feature warm flour tortilla wraps wrapped around an Extra Crispy chicken tender. Available varieties include the Classic Wrap (pickles & mayo), Spicy Slaw Wrap (spicy sauce & coleslaw), Honey BBQ Wrap (honey BBQ sauce & pickles), and the Mac & Cheese Wrap (tender topped with creamy cheddar mac & cheese).

Nutrition & Customization Options

KFC sandwiches can be customized to match your dietary preferences:

  • Hold the Mayo / Sauce: Reduces total calorie count by approximately 140 to 160 calories.
  • Extra Pickles: Complimentary upon request at most drive-thrus and in the mobile app.
  • Allergen Notice: Sandwiches contain wheat (gluten), milk, eggs (in mayo), and soy. Fried in high-grade vegetable shortening.
