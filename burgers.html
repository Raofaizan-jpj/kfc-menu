KFC Burgers & Chicken Sandwiches: Menu Breakdown & Recipes

While known internationally as KFC burgers, KFC's sandwich lineup in the United States is crafted around an extra-thick, 100% white-meat chicken breast filet. Every filet is double-hand-breaded in KFC's signature crispy coating to deliver a crunch that locks in real tenderness and juices:

Top Favorite

KFC Classic Chicken Sandwich

$5.99

Double-breaded extra-crispy breast filet, 3 thick crinkle-cut pickles, and Colonel's real mayo on a toasted, buttered brioche bun.

  • Calories: ~650 kcal
  • Protein: 34g
  • Combo Price: $9.49 (with Fries & Medium Drink)

KFC Spicy Chicken Sandwich (Zinger Style)

$5.99

The American counterpart to the global KFC Zinger Burger. Features a spicy double-breaded breast filet, signature fiery pepper sauce, and pickles on brioche.

  • Calories: ~650 kcal
  • Heat Level: Bold spicy kick
  • Combo Price: $9.49 (with Fries & Medium Drink)

Bacon & Cheese Chicken Sandwich

$6.99

A loaded upgrade featuring the crispy breast filet topped with hickory-smoked bacon strips and melted Monterey Jack cheese.

  • Calories: ~740 kcal
  • Toppings: Smoked Bacon, Monterey Jack, Pickles, Mayo
  • Combo Price: $10.49

KFC Chicken Littles (Mini Burger)

$2.79

Snack-sized mini chicken burger featuring an Extra Crispy chicken tender, mayo, and pickles on a soft sesame seed slider bun.

  • Calories: ~300 kcal
  • Value Offer: 2 for $5.00 Deal
  • Combo Price: $7.49 (2 Littles + Side + Drink)

KFC Warm Tortilla Wraps & Twister Options (2 for $5.00 Deal)

Looking for portable wraps and twister options? KFC restaurants in the US feature warm flour tortilla wraps wrapped around an Extra Crispy chicken tender. Available varieties include the Classic Wrap (pickles & mayo), Spicy Slaw Wrap (spicy sauce & coleslaw), Honey BBQ Wrap (honey BBQ sauce & pickles), and the Mac & Cheese Wrap (tender topped with creamy cheddar mac & cheese), priced at $2.99 each or 2 for $5.00.

Nutrition & Customization Options for KFC Burgers

Every sandwich and burger on the KFC USA menu can be customized to match your dietary preferences:

  • Hold the Mayo / Spicy Sauce: Reduces sandwich calorie count by approximately 140 to 160 kcal.
  • Extra Pickles: Available at no additional charge when ordering through the drive-thru or KFC mobile app.
  • Allergen Notice: Sandwiches contain wheat (gluten), milk, eggs (in mayo), and soy. Fried in high-grade vegetable shortening.

Frequently Asked Questions About KFC Burgers & Sandwiches

In the United States, KFC burgers are officially titled chicken sandwiches. The core lineup includes:
  • KFC Classic Chicken Sandwich: Double-breaded breast fillet with Colonel's mayo and pickles ($5.99).
  • KFC Spicy Chicken Sandwich: Fiery double-breaded breast fillet with spicy sauce and pickles ($5.99).
  • Bacon & Cheese Chicken Sandwich: Loaded with hickory-smoked bacon and Monterey Jack cheese ($6.99).
  • Chicken Littles: Crispy tender slider with mayo and pickles on a sesame bun ($2.79 or 2 for $5.00).
The KFC Zinger Burger is KFC's world-famous spicy chicken burger. In the United States, the exact same flavor and recipe is served as the KFC Spicy Chicken Sandwich, featuring an extra-crispy double-breaded spicy fillet, signature spicy mayo sauce, and thick pickles on a buttered brioche bun for $5.99.
The standalone Classic and Spicy sandwiches cost $5.99 a la carte across most US restaurants. A complete combo meal with Secret Recipe Fries and a medium fountain beverage is priced at $9.49. The Bacon & Cheese version is $6.99 ($10.49 combo), and Chicken Littles are $2.79 each.
A KFC Chicken Sandwich Combo includes your choice of sandwich (Classic, Spicy, or Bacon & Cheese), 1 individual homestyle side (such as Secret Recipe Fries, Mashed Potatoes with Gravy, or Coleslaw), and 1 medium fountain beverage (such as Pepsi, Mountain Dew Sweet Lightning, or Starry).
International burger favorites such as the double-patty Mighty Zinger Burger and the value Krunch Burger correspond to the loaded Bacon & Cheese Chicken Sandwich ($6.99) and crispy snack-sized Chicken Littles ($2.79) on the US menu, alongside Kentucky Fried Chicken Wraps (2 for $5.00).
KFC warm tortilla wraps (the US evolution of the classic KFC Twister) are priced at $2.99 individually or as part of a popular 2-for-$5.00 deal. Varieties include Classic, Spicy Slaw, Honey BBQ, and Mac & Cheese wraps.
