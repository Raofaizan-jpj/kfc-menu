Why Choose a KFC Signature Box or Big Box Meal?

While standard combos include 1 individual side, a KFC signature box (or Big Box Meal) doubles your side portions, allowing you to pair two distinct homestyle favorites (such as Secret Recipe Fries AND Mashed Potatoes with Gravy) alongside generous protein servings:

Value Comparison: Combo vs Signature Box

For an incremental difference of approximately $1.50 to $2.00 over a standard combo, a Big Box adds a second complete homestyle side (valued at $3.29 a la carte), delivering unmatched per-dollar value.
