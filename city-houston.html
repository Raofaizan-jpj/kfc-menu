Ordering KFC in Houston: Texas Sales Tax & Family Value

In the Houston metroplex, family bucket dining and southern comfort pairings are staples. Essential local ordering information:

  • Texas Sales Tax: Orders in Houston and Harris County are subject to the standard 8.25% Texas state and local sales tax (6.25% state + 2.0% local).
  • Sweet Tea & Sweet Lightning: Houston outlets are known for generous half-gallon drink bags of Southern Sweet Iced Tea and fountain Sweet Lightning.
  • Drive-Thru Hours: Key suburban branches along I-10 Katy Freeway and Westheimer Rd operate drive-thrus until 1:00 AM on weekends.
