🍗 Authentic Colonel's Heritage & Cooking Methods

Original Recipe vs Extra Crispy: The Secret Behind the Flavor

At KFC USA, fried chicken is prepared fresh daily using two distinct, time-tested culinary techniques:

Colonel's Classic

Original Recipe® Chicken

11 Herbs & Spices

Cooked in specialized commercial pressure fryers. The pressure seals in the natural juices of the chicken while infusing the proprietary blend of 11 herbs and spices deep into the meat.

  • Texture: Tender, juicy with savory spiced skin
  • Cooking Method: Pressure-frying
  • Calories (per Breast): ~390 kcal

Extra Crispy™ Chicken

Double Breaded

Double-dipped and double-breaded by hand, then fried in open vats. This creates the famous craggy, golden-brown crust with an unmistakable audible crunch in every bite.

  • Texture: Ultra-crispy, golden crunch
  • Cooking Method: Open-vat frying
  • Calories (per Breast): ~530 kcal

Hand-Breaded Tenders

100% White Meat

Cut from tenderloin whole-muscle chicken breast, marinated, and hand-breaded in Extra Crispy coating. Served with your choice of KFC dipping sauces.

  • Combos: 3-Piece ($8.99), 5-Piece ($11.49)
  • Box Meal: 4-Piece Big Box ($12.49)
  • Calories (3 Tenders): ~420 kcal

KFC Saucy Nuggets & Glazed Wings: Hand-Tossed Flavors

KFC's 100% white-meat chicken nuggets and crispy wings are hand-tossed to order in a selection of rich, flavorful sauces:

🍯 Honey BBQ: Sweet brown sugar, honey, and smoky hickory notes.
🌶️ Mango Habanero: Tropical fruity sweetness with a fiery habanero kick.
🥢 Korean BBQ: Umami-rich soy, sesame, garlic, and sweet red chili.
🔥 Honey Sriracha: Sweet clover honey infused with fermented chili heat.

Chicken Cuts & Piece Selection Guide

When ordering chicken combos or buckets at KFC, you can customize your preferred cuts:

🍗 Drumstick: Juicy dark meat with rich flavor (~130 kcal Original / ~170 kcal Extra Crispy).
🍗 Thigh: The juiciest cut with tender dark meat (~280 kcal Original / ~330 kcal Extra Crispy).
🍗 Breast: Premium 100% white meat with high protein (~390 kcal Original / ~530 kcal Extra Crispy).
🍗 Wing: Crisp, savory appetizer cut (~130 kcal Original / ~170 kcal Extra Crispy).
