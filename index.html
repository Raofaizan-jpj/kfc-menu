Burger Spotlight

Zinger Stacker Price

Complete breakdown of current standalone pricing, combo meal inclusions, and key differences between the Zinger Stacker and the classic Zinger Burger.

What is the KFC Zinger Stacker?

The Zinger Stacker is engineered for burger lovers who find a single patty insufficient. It features two golden, spicy Zinger chicken fillets layered with a slice of melted cheddar cheese, zesty pickled jalapenos, and KFC’s signature tangy stacker sauce—all packed inside a warm, freshly toasted sesame bun. The combination of intense crunch, heat, and melted cheese has made it one of the most ordered items on the KFC Pakistan menu.

Standalone Zinger Stacker Burger

Rs. 660 (≈ $2.36 USD)

Just the burger by itself—ideal if you already have drinks or sides, or want a high-protein spicy lunch on the go.

  • 2x Spicy Crunchy Chicken Fillets
  • Melted Cheddar Cheese Slice
  • Pickled Jalapenos & Stacker Sauce
  • Toasted Sesame Bun

Location Guide • Search Query Clarification

KFC Sector F-1 Menu

Clarifying Sector F-1 search queries: Details on the Sector F-1 Mirpur branch and Islamabad sector branch alternatives.

Understanding the "KFC Sector F-1" Search Query

Many Pakistani diners searching online for "KFC Sector F-1 menu" are looking for one of two distinct locations:

  1. The Established Branch in Sector F-1, New Mirpur (Azad Kashmir): Located at 53-Main Kotli Road, Sector F-1, Mirpur. This is an official regional branch serving residents of Mirpur and surrounding Azad Jammu & Kashmir areas.
  2. Islamabad Residents Searching for 'F' Sectors: In Islamabad, the Capital Development Authority (CDA) sector grid begins from F-5, F-6, and F-7 upwards; there is no residential "Sector F-1" in Islamabad. Visitors searching for F-1 in the capital are typically seeking nearby active Islamabad branches in Sector F-10 Markaz, Sector F-11 Markaz, Sector F-7 Jinnah Super, or Sector F-6 Super Market.

1. Mirpur Branch: Sector F-1

Address: 53-Main Kotli Road, Sector F-1, New Mirpur, Azad Kashmir.
Services: Dine-in, Takeaway, Car Hop & Home Delivery.
Hours: Generally 11:00 AM – 1:00 AM daily.

Serves the standard KFC Pakistan menu, including Krunch Burgers (from Rs. 330), Zinger Burgers (around Rs. 600), Crispy Chicken Buckets, and Family Deals.

2. Islamabad Alternative 'F' Sector Outlets

Nearby Outlets: F-10 Markaz, F-11 Markaz, F-7 Jinnah Super, F-6 Super Market, Centaurus Mall.
Services: 24/7 or late night drive-thru, delivery up to 3:00 AM.
Hours: 11:00 AM – 3:00 AM.

Offers the full capital menu lineup including midnight deals, Zinger Stacker Combos, and express delivery across Islamabad CDA sectors.

Menu Categories & Pricing Information

Whether visiting the Sector F-1 Mirpur branch or any Islamabad sector outlet, base item prices conform to standard KFC Pakistan menu rates:

  • Individual Burgers: Krunch Burger (Rs. 330), Zinger Burger (Rs. 600), Zinger Stacker (Rs. 660), Mighty Zinger (Rs. 770).
  • Crispy Fried Chicken: 1 Piece (Rs. 320), 3 Pieces (Rs. 890), 5 Pieces (Rs. 1,450).
  • Meal Combos: Burger + fries + soft drink (Rs. 590 to Rs. 950).
  • Feast Buckets: 9 Pcs Family Bucket (Rs. 2,290), Family Festival 1 (Rs. 2,450).
