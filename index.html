How Much is a KFC Chicken Sandwich & Family Meal?

Two of the most frequently searched questions about the kfc full menu with price are the cost of the flagship chicken sandwich and large family buckets:

🥪 KFC Chicken Sandwich Prices

The KFC Classic Chicken Sandwich and KFC Spicy Chicken Sandwich are priced at $5.99 standalone.

  • A La Carte Sandwich: $5.99
  • Sandwich Combo (with Fries & Drink): $9.49
  • Bacon & Cheese Sandwich: $6.99
  • Chicken Little Slider: $2.79 (or 2 for $5)

🪣 How Much is the KFC 12 Piece Meal?

The 12 Pc Family Feast Meal is standard at $33.99 across the USA.

  • 12 Pc Family Meal (3 Large Sides + 6 Biscuits): $33.99
  • 12 Pc Bucket (Chicken Only): $25.99
  • 8 Pc Family Meal (2 Large Sides + 4 Biscuits): $24.99
  • 16 Pc Mega Feast (4 Large Sides + 8 Biscuits): $41.99

Frequently Asked Questions — KFC USA Menu & Prices

Clear answers to top user queries about KFC costs, bucket sizes, sandwich pricing, and delivery.

Individual meals and combos at KFC USA range between $8.99 and $11.49, including an entree (chicken pieces, tenders, or sandwich), an individual side (fries, mashed potatoes, or coleslaw), a warm biscuit, and a medium beverage. Standalone snack items cost $2.49 to $3.49, while multi-person family bucket feasts range from $20.00 to $41.99.
The 12-piece Family Feast meal is approximately $33.99. It comes with 12 pieces of freshly fried chicken, 3 large homestyle sides, and 6 warm buttermilk biscuits. If you only want the chicken without sides, the 12-piece bucket is priced at $25.99.
The KFC Chicken Sandwich (Classic or Spicy with extra-crispy chicken breast, crinkle-cut pickles, and mayo or spicy sauce on a brioche bun) costs $5.99 a la carte. The full combo with Secret Recipe Fries and a medium drink is $9.49.
A 3-piece hand-breaded Extra Crispy tenders combo is $8.99 with fries, biscuit, dipping sauce, and a medium drink. The 5-piece tenders combo is $11.49, and the 4-piece Tenders Big Box Meal with two sides is $12.49.
The KFC Famous Bowl combines a foundation of creamy whipped mashed potatoes topped with sweet whole kernel corn, crispy bite-sized chicken nuggets, rich savory brown gravy, and a blend of three shredded cheeses, priced at $6.99.
Yes, KFC delivery is available nationwide across thousands of locations via the official KFC App, kfc.com, DoorDash, Uber Eats, and Grubhub. Many US metropolitan locations offer late-night delivery until 1:00 AM or 2:00 AM.
