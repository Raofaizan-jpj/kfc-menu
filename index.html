Frequently Asked Questions — KFC USA Menu & Prices

Detailed answers about KFC costs, burgers, wings, buckets, combos, and local branch availability.

The full KFC menu in the USA includes signature bone-in fried chicken (Original Recipe and Extra Crispy), KFC chicken sandwiches and burgers, hand-breaded Extra Crispy tenders, Saucy Nuggets, Kentucky fried chicken wings, KFC Famous Bowls, Chunky Chicken Pot Pies, multi-piece family feast buckets (8, 12, and 16 pieces), and southern homestyle sides such as Secret Recipe Fries, mashed potatoes with gravy, coleslaw, mac & cheese, and buttermilk biscuits. Standalone items start at $2.49, combos range from $8.99 to $11.49, and complete family feast buckets range between $18.99 and $41.99.
For an individual diner, a full KFC combo meal (entree, individual side, biscuit, and beverage) typically costs between $8.99 and $11.49. Value deals such as the Taste of KFC 2-piece meal cost $4.99. For families or small groups, sharing a bucket meal costs between $20.00 and $41.99, averaging roughly $5.00 to $7.00 per person.
In the USA, KFC chicken sandwiches are celebrated nationwide as the brand's signature burgers. Top options include the KFC Classic Chicken Sandwich (extra-crispy double-breaded chicken breast fillet with mayo and pickles on a brioche bun for $5.99), the KFC Spicy Chicken Sandwich (the US equivalent of the famous KFC Zinger Burger for $5.99), the Bacon & Cheese Chicken Sandwich ($6.99), and snack-sized Chicken Littles ($2.79).
The KFC Zinger Burger is famous globally for its fiery, double-breaded crispy chicken fillet, spicy sauce, and lettuce or pickles. In US locations, this exact flavor profile is officially served as the KFC Spicy Chicken Sandwich, featuring a 100% white-meat spicy double-breaded breast fillet with spicy signature sauce and thick crinkle-cut pickles on a toasted brioche bun for $5.99 a la carte ($9.49 as a combo with fries and a drink).
International burger favorites such as the double-fillet Mighty Zinger Burger and the value Krunch Burger correspond to the loaded Bacon & Cheese Chicken Sandwich ($6.99) and crispy snack-sized Chicken Littles ($2.79) on the US menu, alongside Kentucky Fried Chicken Wraps (2 for $5.00).
KFC bucket prices depend on piece count and side inclusions:
  • 8-Piece Meal: $24.99 with 2 large sides and 4 biscuits ($18.99 chicken only)
  • 12-Piece Family Feast: $33.99 with 3 large sides and 6 biscuits ($25.99 chicken only)
  • 16-Piece Mega Feast: $41.99 with 4 large sides and 8 biscuits ($32.99 chicken only)
Yes, KFC offers crispy bone-in wings (available in Original Recipe and Extra Crispy) and Saucy Glazed Nuggets and Wings tossed in Honey BBQ, Korean BBQ, or Mango Habanero. Wings are sold in meal combos ($8.99 to $10.99), as add-ons to chicken buckets, and in special promotional party packs where available.
A standard KFC Combo comes with 1 main entree (such as a Chicken Sandwich, 2-pc chicken, or 3-pc tenders), 1 individual side (fries, mashed potatoes, or coleslaw), a warm biscuit, and a medium fountain drink. A KFC Big Box Meal (or signature box) includes 2 individual sides along with a hearty portion of chicken or tenders, biscuit, and beverage for $12.49 to $12.99. Two-person meal bundles (Duo Box / 2 Can Dine deals) feature 2 sandwiches or 4 chicken pieces with 2 sides, 2 biscuits, and 2 drinks for around $16.99 to $18.99.
Kentucky Fried Chicken Wraps (the US evolution of the classic KFC Twister) are priced at $2.99 each or as a 2 for $5.00 value deal, featuring an Extra Crispy chicken tender rolled in a warm flour tortilla with sauce, pickles, coleslaw, or mac and cheese.
KFC fountain cold drinks (Pepsi, Diet Pepsi, MTN DEW Sweet Lightning, Starry, Sweet Iced Tea) cost $2.69 for a Medium (20 oz) and $2.99 for a Large (30 oz). Half-Gallon beverage bags for family meals are priced at $4.99.
