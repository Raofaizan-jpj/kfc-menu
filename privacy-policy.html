Privacy Policy

How user information and privacy are protected on KFC USA Menu Guide.

📅 Last Updated: September 25, 2026

1. Informational Service Overview

KFC USA Menu Guide (kfc-menu-orpin.vercel.app) operates as an independent educational resource that catalogues American KFC menu items, nationwide calorie counts, and estimated USD pricing lists. We do not process restaurant food orders, take payments, or require account creation to browse our guides.

2. Information We Collect

When you visit our site, our hosting infrastructure and analytics tools (Google Analytics 4) may log standard non-personally identifiable diagnostic information such as your anonymized IP address, operating system, browser type, referral URLs, and pages visited. This data is utilized solely to analyze aggregate traffic patterns and enhance user navigation.

3. Cookies & Local Browser Storage

We may use lightweight browser cookies and localStorage keys to store your on-site preferences (such as price filter settings, city search queries, and interface display modes). You can clear or disable cookies at any time through your browser preferences.

4. Third-Party Links & External Services

Our pages feature outbound links to official ordering platforms (kfc.com), delivery services (DoorDash, Uber Eats, Grubhub), and social media platforms. We do not control or endorse the privacy practices of any third-party websites and urge you to review their respective privacy policies.

5. Contact and Inquiries

If you have questions regarding this privacy policy or our data practices, please reach out through our Contact Page or review our Editorial Policy.
