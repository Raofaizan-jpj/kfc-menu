Ordering KFC in New York City: Taxes, Delivery & Pick-Up

Dining at KFC in NYC is popular with commuters, students, and tourists. Here are essential tips for ordering across the Five Boroughs:

  • NYC Sales Tax: Fast food purchases in New York City are subject to the combined 8.875% NYC and NY State sales tax, calculated at checkout.
  • Quick Pick-Up Shelves: To skip midtown lunch lines near Times Square or Fulton St, order ahead in the KFC App for express shelf grab-and-go.
  • Late Night Delivery: Manhattan and Brooklyn branches deliver via DoorDash, Uber Eats, and Grubhub until 2:00 AM on weekends.
